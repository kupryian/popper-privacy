Privacy Policy

Popper — a game about skincare procedures
Effective date: 4 September 2026

The short version. Popper collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. It does not even hold permission to use the internet: the only permission it requests is the one that makes your phone vibrate. Everything you do in the game is written to your own device and stays there.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Popper (the “Game”), published by spaceapk (“we”, “us”). It applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account. There is no sign-in and no profile. The Game contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

We operate no servers. There is nowhere for your information to be sent, and nothing of yours for us to hold.

2. What the Game stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally, in the Game's own private storage:

  • the name you gave your salon;
  • your crystals, stars and visitor count;
  • which notes you have collected and which story scenes you have played;
  • your settings: language, sound and music volume, hints, tutorial.

None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device. It is removed completely when you uninstall the Game or clear its data through Android's settings. Because we never receive it, we cannot restore it for you — and there is nothing for us to delete on request, since uninstalling is the deletion.

3. Permissions

The Game requests a single Android permission: vibration, used for the small buzz when a procedure goes wrong.

It does not request internet access, and therefore cannot transmit anything anywhere. It does not request the camera, microphone, contacts, files, location, or any other permission.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current version of the Game contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. We never see payment information of any kind.

If this changes. We intend to add optional rewarded video adverts — ones you choose to watch in exchange for in-game currency. Should that happen, an advertising network (Google AdMob) would collect device information such as an advertising identifier and IP address in order to serve the advert. This page will be updated before such a version is released, the Google Play data safety section will be updated with it, and players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before any personalised advert is shown.

5. Children

Popper is not directed to children. It depicts realistic skin procedures, including blood, and is rated accordingly in Google Play. Since the Game collects no information at all, no information is collected from children either.

6. Your rights

Data protection laws — including the GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA in California — give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability.

These rights are meaningful only where there is data to exercise them against. We hold none, and we process none: there is no personal data to access, export or erase, and there is nothing for us to sell or share. We do not sell or share personal information, for money or otherwise.

If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will answer. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date at the top. Any change that affects what data is collected will be published before the version that makes it is released.
